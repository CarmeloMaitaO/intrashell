# Contributing to Intrashell

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Intrashell! This document provides guidelines and information about contributing to this project.

## Communication

- **GitHub Discussions**: Use the Discussions tab for questions, ideas, and general conversation.

## How to Contribute

### Reporting Bugs

If you find a bug, please open an issue with the following information:

- A clear and descriptive title
- Steps to reproduce the behavior
- Expected behavior
- Actual behavior
- Screenshots or logs (if applicable)
- Your environment (OS, browser, version, etc.)

## Thank You

Thank you for taking the time to contribute to Intrashell! Every contribution, no matter how small, makes a difference.
